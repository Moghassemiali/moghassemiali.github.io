I am a Post-Doctoral Research Associate at the <a href="https://uwm.edu/" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee </a>, working with Dr. Robert Cuzner at <a href="https://sites.uwm.edu/sees/" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> Center for Sustainable Electrical Energy Systems (SEES)</a>. I completed my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at <a href="https://www.clemson.edu/" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> Clemson University </a>, advised by Dr. Christopher Edrington at <a href="https://eciclemson.wixsite.com/mysite" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> Real Time COntrols and Optimization Laboratory (RT-COOL)</a>, where I was honored to receive two **Outstanding Graduate Researcher** awards from the <a href="https://www.clemson.edu/cecas/departments/ece/" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> Holcombe Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering</a> (department-wide award) and also from <a href="https://www.clemson.edu/graduate/" style="color: #1E90FF; text-decoration: none;"> Graduate School</a> (university-wide award) at Clemson University.

My research focuses on the design, modeling, and control of intelligent, advanced power electronics converters for transportation electrification, renewable energy systems, and power system applications.

In my free time, I enjoy movies and TV series 🎬, kitchen experiments 🍳, and an ongoing quest for the perfect cup of coffee ☕; my favorite closed-loop control system.
